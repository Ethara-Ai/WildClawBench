MODULE 3 — FIELD SAFETY
Citizen Water-Monitoring Certification Program
Doña Ana County Environmental Services

1. Purpose
This module covers safety protocols for all field-based water-monitoring activities conducted under the Citizen Water-Monitoring Certification Program.

2. Scope
Applies to all certified volunteers, field coordinators, and supervising staff during sampling events at designated monitoring sites (Sites 1–12).

3. General Safety Requirements
- All volunteers must complete this module before participating in any field sampling.
- Minimum volunteer age: 18
- Volunteers must wear closed-toe shoes, long pants, and sun protection at all times in the field.
- A first-aid kit must be present at every sampling site.

4. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Nitrile gloves (provided)
- Safety glasses when handling reagents
- High-visibility vest when working near roadways

5. Site Hazards — Southwestern Riparian
- Flash-flood awareness: do not sample if upstream thunderstorms are visible or forecast.
- Wildlife: be aware of rattlesnakes, scorpions, and fire ants near riverbanks.
- Heat illness: hydrate frequently; sampling sessions should not exceed 4 hours without a shaded rest break.

6. Emergency Procedures
- Dial 911 for life-threatening emergencies.
- Notify the field coordinator immediately for any injury, near-miss, or equipment failure.
- Incident reports must be filed within 24 hours using Form FS-IR-01.

7. Chemical Handling
- All reagents are EPA-approved citizen-science grade.
- Refer to Safety Data Sheets (SDS) stored in each sampling kit.
- Dispose of used reagents in provided neutralization bags; never pour into waterways.
