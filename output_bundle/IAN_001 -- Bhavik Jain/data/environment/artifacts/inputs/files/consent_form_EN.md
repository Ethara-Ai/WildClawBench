# INFORMED CONSENT FORM

**Citizen Water-Monitoring Certification Program**
**Doña Ana County Environmental Services**

---

## §1 Purpose

You are invited to participate in the Citizen Water-Monitoring Certification Program administered by Doña Ana County Environmental Services in partnership with Ridgemont State University. The purpose of this program is to train community volunteers in standardized water-quality sampling methods so that collected data meets EPA citizen-science accreditation standards.

## §2 What You'll Do

If you agree to participate, you will:
- Attend a multi-module training curriculum covering water chemistry, field safety, sampling techniques, and data reporting.
- Participate in supervised field-sampling sessions at designated monitoring sites along the Rio Grande corridor in Doña Ana County.
- Record water-quality measurements (pH, turbidity, conductivity) using provided equipment.
- Submit sampling data through the program's secure data-collection platform.

Your participation is expected to span approximately 4 days of training and 6 monthly sampling events.

## §3 Risks

Participation in this program involves minimal risk. Potential risks include:
- Minor physical discomfort from outdoor fieldwork (heat, sun exposure, uneven terrain).
- Possible skin irritation from handling water-testing reagents (nitrile gloves are provided).
- Risk of slips, trips, or falls near waterways.

All field activities are supervised by trained coordinators, and safety protocols are covered in Module 3 (Field Safety).

## §4 Compensation

Participants will receive:
- A certificate of completion upon finishing the training curriculum.
- A water-monitoring field kit valued at approximately $85 for personal use after program completion.
- No monetary compensation is provided for participation.

## §5 Participant Rights

As a participant in this program, you have the following rights:
- Right to ask questions about the program at any time.
- Right to refuse to perform any sampling activity you consider unsafe.
- Right to withdraw at any time without penalty.
- Right to request that your personal data be removed from program records upon withdrawal.
- Right to receive a copy of any data you personally collected.

## §6 Confidentiality

Your personal information (name, contact details, demographic data) will be kept confidential and stored in a password-protected database accessible only to program staff. Sampling data you collect will be de-identified before inclusion in any published reports or datasets. Your name will not appear in any publication without your written consent.
