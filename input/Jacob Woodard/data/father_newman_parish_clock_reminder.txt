REMINDER — Father Newman, parish wall clock service
Parish: Corpus Christi, Kazimierz (the small Lady chapel side, not the nave)
Frequency: bi-monthly, first Monday
Owner of reminder: Jacob

The clock
- Late 19th-century Vienna regulator, plain mahogany case, brass-faced dial
- Single weight, deadbeat escapement, eight-day run with a comfortable margin
- Mainspring not relevant — weight-driven
- Beats well in cold weather, drifts five seconds a day in summer humidity

Standard service (under thirty minutes, no parts ordinarily)
- Wind, set to the church radio time signal, listen for the strike
- Light pivot inspection, no oil unless ten months overdue
- Pendulum suspension spring inspection by eye, replace only if visibly creased
- Check the case foot is square to the wall, the south wall lists a hair after spring

Standing notes
- Father Newman appreciates the visit and the tea afterward. Do not rush him.
- If he asks about the Friday Mass schedule clock in the sacristy, that one is his nephew's. Decline politely.
- Parish pays in coffee and good biscuits. No invoice. This is gratis since 1996 and stays gratis.
