DRAFT — birthday card for Henry, 67th
(handwrite on the linen card stock, do not send from Gmail)

Henry,

Sixty-seven. You wear it the way you wear a worn case-back — comfortably and with the patina earned. Ellen and I would like to have you to ourselves for a quiet supper at the workshop, the Tuesday after your birthday if your knee will let you walk to Józefa. Pierogi and the Tokaj you complained about last year, which I now keep stocked specifically to hear you complain about it again.

I have the marine chronometer you asked me about, the one from the 1962 lot. It is on the bench under glass. Bring your loupe.

Steady hands,
Jacob

— card stock from the Florianska shop, not the cheap one
— pierogi from Pod Wawelem, ring Ewa by Saturday
— Tokaj 2017, two bottles, the dry one
