DRAFT — anniversary card for Ellen
(handwrite on the heavy ivory stock, leave on her side of the table the night before)

Ellen,

Thirty-two years next month, and somehow you still find it in yourself to put up with a man who measures the kitchen in seconds per day and sighs at the Junghans every time it gains a quarter. You have lived among ticking clocks for longer than the country has had its current shape. That is not a small thing to ask of anyone.

I have booked us a quiet table at Pod Aniolami for the Saturday after the date. Not the Friday. Friday I owe Mark a bench session and you would never forgive me for cutting it short. The Saturday lets us walk the long way home along Grodzka.

The roses, the white ones. As always.

Yours,
Jacob

Reminders for myself:
- Pod Aniolami booking, ring Tomek
- white roses from the Florianska shop, not the market stall
- card on her side the night before, do not leave it on top of the parts ledger
