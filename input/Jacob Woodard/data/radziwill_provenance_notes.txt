WH-2026-031 — RADZIWILL POCKET WATCH, PROVENANCE WORKING NOTES
Owner: Kraków City Museum (loan custody)
Collaborator: Prof Andrew Mitchell, Jagiellonian University
Deadline: December 2026 (museum board review)
Filed: ulica Józefa workshop, working file W-031

Movement summary
- 1780s Polish-Lithuanian verge escapement, gilt brass full plate
- Diameter 56 mm, double-bottom enamel dial (hairline crack at 4 o'clock)
- Pierced and engraved cock with three-armed monogram (unidentified)
- Cylindrical mainspring barrel, fusee chain intact, replacement chain hooks 1890s

Provenance chain (working, NOT for publication)
- Workshop of unknown Vilnius journeyman, c.1782-1789 (regional school attribution only)
- Private collection, Nieśwież line, 19th-c. inventory entries reference "watch with cipher"
- Twentieth-century gap: 1939-1945 lost, 1956 surfaces in Warsaw private hands
- Museum acquisition 1971, accession PL-MZK-1971-114
- Conservation last performed 1998 by Henryk Lasocki (deceased 2014)
