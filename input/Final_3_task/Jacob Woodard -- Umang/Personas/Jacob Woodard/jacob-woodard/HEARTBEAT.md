# Heartbeat: Jacob Woodard

## Recurring Events

### Daily

- **Daily, 5:30 AM**: Wake. Forty-five minute walk along the Vistula, in nearly all weather. Strong coffee on return.
- **Daily, 10:00 PM**: Bed. Light sleeper, 7-hour target.

### Weekly (Weekdays)

- **Monday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM**: Weekly admin block. Review invoices, parts order status, update client log. Afternoon: supply runs, errands, or meeting Henry. No client appointments.
- **Tuesday, 8:30 AM**: Precision work prep. Check workshop humidity and temperature. Lay out morning restoration tools. Workshop opens 9:00 AM, client consultations and Mark's training in afternoon.
- **Wednesday, 12:30 PM**: Lunch break at The Corner Diner. Afternoon focused restoration. Workshop open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- **Thursday, 9:00 AM**: Supplier call window. Client pickups and drop-offs concentrated through the day. Workshop open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- **Friday, 12:15 PM**: Head to The Linden Table. Lunch with Henry at 12:30 PM. Lighter afternoon, Mark handles routine repairs, Jacob reviews the week before close.

### Weekly (Weekend)

- **Saturday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM**: Half-day at workshop, finish urgent pieces. Café with Ellen at Botanica, 3:30 PM, usual corner table. Evening varies, sometimes family dinner if Katherine visiting.
- **Sunday, 9:00 AM**: Mass at St. Catherine's, third row left. Visual inspection of the church clock from the nave. Family dinner at noon. Afternoon rest, reading, or Ojców hike if weather permits.

### Biweekly

- **Every other Wednesday, 6:30 PM**: Kraków Horological Guild meeting on Floriańska Street.

### Monthly

- **First Monday of each month**: QuickBooks close. Reconcile Square, Stripe, PayPal, and PKO Bank Polski deposits.
- **1st of each month**: Review bank account, monthly budget, and supplier outstanding balances.
- **15th of each month**: Verify workshop insurance auto-payment processed.

### Quarterly

- **Quarterly**: St. Catherine's tower clock service and seasonal regulation. Coordinated with Father Newman.
- **Quarterly**: Stefan Müller order review. Lead times, outstanding invoices, parts on the way.
- **Quarterly**: Mark's training milestone review with the Chamber of Crafts log.

### Seasonal / Variable

- **Every six months**: Ophthalmology appointment with Dr. Stępień. Critical for the work.
- **Warmer months, most weekends**: Hike in the Tatras or Ojców National Park, weather permitting. Cold-weather day-trips occasionally in winter.

### Annual

- **February**: Annual checkup with Dr. Wilk at Centrum Medyczne Kazimierz.
- **Last Thursday of November**: Thanksgiving dinner at the apartment. Ellen's cornbread stuffing is the centerpiece.
- **December 24**: Wigilia. Twelve-dish supper at home with the family.
- **December 25**: Christmas Mass at St. Catherine's, then a quiet day at home.
- **July 4**: Fourth of July BBQ at home with Ellen.
- **August**: Family week in Zakopane with extended family.
- **Easter weekend**: Holy Week observance at St. Catherine's and family meal at home.
