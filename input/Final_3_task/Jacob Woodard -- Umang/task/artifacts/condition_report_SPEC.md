The watch is in good overall condition for its age. The movement is largely intact with original components. The case shows expected patina and minor wear. ### Section 5 — Material Analysis

Based on visual and loupe examination (10x, 20x):

- **Case**: Gilt brass construction consistent with late 18th century Polish/German production methods. Fire-gilding technique visible under magnification.
- **Movement plates**: Brass, hand-finished with file marks visible. Period-correct production.
- **Steel components**: Carbon steel pinions, pivots, and springs. Bluing on balance cock screws consistent with period techniques.
- **Dial**: White enamel over copper substrate. Roman numerals hand-painted. Two hairline cracks radiating from centre — likely from thermal stress.
- **Dating assessment**: "Consistent with late 18th century Polish/German production." Material composition, construction techniques, and engraving style all support a date range of c. 1780–1800.

### Section 7 — Restoration Recommendations

1. **Hairspring re-forming** — Straighten slight set. Estimate: 3 hours.
2. **Mainspring evaluation** — Test under load. Replace if tension below 1.5N. Estimate: 2 hours + part if needed.
3. **Dial stabilisation** — Consolidate hairline cracks with conservation-grade adhesive. Do NOT re-enamel. Estimate: 4 hours.
4. **Case cleaning** — Light cleaning of gilt brass. Do NOT re-gild. Preserve patina. Silver cartouche tarnish removal. Estimate: 2 hours.
5. **Movement service** — Full disassembly, ultrasonic cleaning, lubrication with Moebius 9010/9020. Estimate: 8 hours.
6. **Crystal replacement** — Optional. Current replacement crystal is period-appropriate (19th century). Recommend leaving in place.

**Total estimated restoration hours**: 19–22 hours
**Estimated cost**: PLN 3,500–4,200 (excluding parts)
