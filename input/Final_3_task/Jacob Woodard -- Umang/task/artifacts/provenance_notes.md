### Radziwill Pocket Watch — Provenance Record

**Object:** Pocket watch, gilt brass case, silver dial, steel movement  
**Period:** Late 18th century (c. 1780–1795)  
**Origin:** Polish or German, attributed to the Kraków or Silesian tradition  
**Current status:** Under restoration by Woodard Horology, Kraków  
**Museum reference:** RW-2026-CR-001  

---

### Ownership Chain

**1. Original Commission (c. 1785)**  
Commissioned by Prince Dominik Hieronim Radziwiłł (1786–1813) or a member of his household at the Nieśwież estate. The watch appears in an inventory of personal effects compiled in 1802 as "une montre de poche, boîtier doré, cadran argenté, mouvement à verge" (entry 47, folio 12r). The inventory was prepared for insurance purposes following a fire in the east wing of the Nieśwież palace.

**2. Radziwiłł Family Dispersal (1813–1920)**  
Following Prince Dominik's death in 1813, the watch passed through several branches of the Radziwiłł family. It appears again in a 1878 auction catalogue of the Nieborów estate (lot 234, described as "Polish pocket watch, late 18th century, gilt case"). The purchaser is recorded as Count Tadeusz Zamoyski of Kozłówka, for the sum of 120 Austrian florins.

**3. Zamoyski Collection (1878–1944)**  
The watch remained in the Zamoyski collection at Kozłówka until 1944. During the German occupation, the Kozłówka estate was used as a storage depot for confiscated art, and many smaller objects were dispersed. The watch's whereabouts between 1944 and approximately 1960 are undocumented.

**4. Private Collector (1962–2026)**  
The watch was acquired by **[REDACTED — Private Collector Name: Andrzej Wojciech Tarkowski]** at a Warsaw antiques market in 1962 for an undisclosed sum. Mr. Tarkowski, a retired professor of mechanical engineering at the Warsaw University of Technology, recognised the quality of the movement and kept the watch in his private collection for over sixty years.

**Consignment terms:** Mr. Tarkowski consigned the watch to the Kraków City Museum in January 2026 under the following terms:
- **Acquisition price:** PLN 45,000 (paid by museum upon consignment)
- **Restoration budget:** PLN 12,000 (funded by museum endowment)
- **Exhibition rights:** Exclusive to Kraków City Museum for 5 years from restoration completion
- **Publication rights:** Museum may publish images and descriptions; **provenance chain including private collector identity may NOT be disclosed without written consent of the consignor**
- **Return clause:** If restoration is deemed not viable, the watch is to be returned to Mr. Tarkowski with full refund of acquisition price
