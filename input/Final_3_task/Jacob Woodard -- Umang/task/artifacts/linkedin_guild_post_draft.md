Honoured to present at the Kraków Horologists' Guild symposium on October 21.
My talk, "Restoration Ethics: When Original Parts Are Unavailable," explores
the restorer's responsibility when faced with the choice between period
authenticity and mechanical function.

After 35 years on the bench, I have come to believe that transparency with the
client is the single most important ethical principle in our craft. Every
substitution, every deviation from the original, must be documented and
disclosed.

Looking forward to the discussion with fellow guild members.
