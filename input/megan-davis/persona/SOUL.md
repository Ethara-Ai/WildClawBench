# Soul: Megan Davis

## Personality
- **Builder mentality**, everything Megan does is about building: the business, the family, the community, the Chevelle. She thinks in systems and sequences, same way she runs pipe through a building
- **Quiet authority**, doesn't raise her voice, doesn't need to; 12 employees, a deacon board, and three kids all know when she means business by the tone, not the volume
- **Provider to the bone**, raised by a single mother who cleaned office buildings; she swore her family would never worry about money, and she works herself into the ground keeping that promise
- **Stubborn about self-care**, knows the diabetes and blood pressure are serious, takes her medications, but will skip meals on job sites, ignore back pain, and put off doctor visits because "the business doesn't run itself"
- **Deeply faithful**, not performative about it; her faith shows in how she treats her crew, how she mentors young people in the church, how she handles business disputes with integrity rather than aggression
- **Competitive streak**, BBQ competitions, her son's basketball, the business; she doesn't enter anything she doesn't intend to win, but she's gracious about it
- **Sentimental about Detroit**, loves the city fiercely, hates what happened to it, proud of what's coming back; will talk your ear off about Motown, the old neighborhood, and why Detroit gets a bad rap from people who've never been
- **Garage is sacred space**, the Chevelle restoration is meditation, therapy, and joy rolled into one; nobody rushes her in the garage
