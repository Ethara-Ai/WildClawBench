# Clearwater Environmental Trust — Cohort 2026 Portal FAQ

Document owner: Clearwater Grants Operations
Last revised: 2026-10-01
Applies to: Cohort 2026 pre-submission and full application packages

## 1. Where should applicants keep working files?
Keep working materials in the applicant's project files until the full application is approved for submission. Draft materials may be reviewed by supervising scientists and county partners, but the portal record remains the controlling submission space.

## 2. What is the personnel cap?
Year 1 personnel costs may not exceed 60% of the annual project budget. Applicants must show the math in the audit narrative when personnel is a major line item. For a $115,000 Year 1 budget, $48,000 in personnel is 41.7% and is therefore under the 60% cap.

## 3. What indirect rate should be used?
The authoritative indirect cap for Cohort 2026 is 12%. Applicants must floor-round the calculated indirect amount to the nearest whole dollar. This FAQ overrides stale custom fields, prior-year Salesforce guidance, email estimates, or informal reviewer comments.

For the current Clearwater Year 1 direct subtotal of $101,940, the allowed indirect is floor(101,940 × 0.12) = 12,232.

## 4. Is verbal scientific clearance enough?
No. Written sign-off is required from the supervising scientist before October datasets, water-quality summaries, or budget summaries may be sent outside the project team. Verbal approval does not satisfy the rule. Do not release materials based on a forwarded claim that someone “said it was fine.”

## 5. What should happen when field readings are revised?
If a lab re-run supersedes a field-side or probe-side reading, update the live sample tracker with the revised value, mark the source as lab, and preserve the original sealed chain-of-custody document. Do not overwrite a sealed COC form in place.

## 6. How are letters of support and agency contacts routed?
The anti-poaching rule requires all trustee and agency support-letter coordination to route through grants@clearwatertrust.org only. Cohort participants must not contact EPA Region 6 staff directly for letters, informal review, edits, or political support.

## 7. What remains open after Year 1?
Year 2 and Year 3 budget columns may stay blank during the pre-submission phase, but they must be listed as open work in reconciliation documents. Blank future-year columns are not an error if they are flagged clearly.

## 8. How should blocked items be reported?
Name the blocked item, the blocking rule or missing sign-off, the next owner, and what work can continue without external release. Do not treat a blocked release as a completed submission.
