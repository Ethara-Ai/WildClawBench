# Doña Ana County Environmental Services - Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

**Section 4: Paid Time Off, Sick Leave, Comp Time**
Revised: FY 2026, effective 2026-07-01

## 4.1 PTO Accrual

Field staff Grade T-3 (Water Quality Technician):

| Years of service | Accrual rate | Annual cap |
|---|---|---|
| 0 - 2 | 4.0 hr / pay period | 96 hr / yr |
| 2 - 5 | 5.0 hr / pay period | 120 hr / yr |
| 5 - 10 | 6.5 hr / pay period | 156 hr / yr |
| 10+ | 8.0 hr / pay period | 192 hr / yr |

Carry-over cap: 80 hours into the following fiscal year. Anything above is paid out at base hourly rate in the first July check.

## 4.2 Sick leave

Earned at 3.0 hr / pay period, no annual cap. Cap on carry-over: 480 hours. Sick leave used for self, spouse, dependent child, or parent.

## 4.3 Comp time (FLSA non-exempt only)

Field staff who exceed 40 hours in a workweek accrue comp time at 1.5x the overtime hours, in lieu of overtime pay, subject to written supervisor pre-approval. Comp time bank capped at 80 hours. Comp time must be used within 90 days of accrual or it is converted to overtime pay.

Note: weather-window emergency callouts (storm-event grab sampling) are exempt from the pre-approval requirement but must be logged within 48 hours.

## 4.4 Bereavement

Three days paid for immediate family, one additional day if travel exceeds 500 miles.

## 4.5 Jury duty

Full pay for the duration of jury service; jury stipend is forfeited to the county per County Code 2-7-114.
