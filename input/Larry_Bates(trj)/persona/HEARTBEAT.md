# Heartbeat: Larry Bates

## Recurring Events

### Daily

- **Off-season, 6:30 AM**: Morning check. Review the day's schedule, weather forecast, brewery status, overnight email and message summary.
- **Brewing season (November through March), 4:30 AM**: Morning check. Same scope as off-season plus fermentation sensor digest from Twilio and PagerDuty.
- **Brewing season, 9:00 PM**: Evening fermentation review. Tank-by-tank status, sensor anomalies, next-day brewhouse plan with Greg.

### Weekly (Weekdays)

- **Monday, 9:00 AM**: Week start review. Weekly schedule, distributor orders, supply chain status, financial items requiring attention.
- **Tuesday afternoon**: Distributor or supplier visit window. Asheville-local meetings preferred; barley farm visits seasonal.
- **Wednesday, 3:00 PM**: Mid-week production check. Bottling and labeling progress, quality checks, inventory status, hot springs visit if schedule allows.
- **Thursday afternoon**: Barley farmer visit window during harvest months, otherwise flexible for errands and research.
- **Friday, 4:00 PM**: Week wrap. Weekly financial review, production summary, next-week planning, then Farmhouse Tavern dinner with Jake Moreland on the first Friday of each month.

### Weekly (Weekend)

- **Saturday, 7:00 AM**: Morning walk with Hana. Protected time, no work interruptions. Regular route, bakery stop.
- **Sunday, 4:00 PM**: Week preparation. Review upcoming week, brewery maintenance needs, family schedule, light reading or beer study afterward.

### Monthly

- **1st of each month**: Financial review. Brewery P&L, personal budget, savings transfer, loan payment, distributor payment tracking.
- **First Friday of each month**: Dinner with Jake Moreland at The Farmhouse Tavern.
- **Every 4 weeks**: Chiropractor appointment in Asheville for lower back and shoulder maintenance.
- **Monthly**: Hot springs visit in Western North Carolina mountains. Recovery practice.

### Quarterly

- **Quarterly**: Distributor review meeting with Erin Whitfield at Blue Ridge Distribution.
- **Quarterly**: Brewers Association regional check-in call.

### Seasonal / Variable

- **November through March**: Brewing season. 4:30 AM starts, 12 to 16 hour days, six days per week.
- **April**: Final bottling of brewing season production. Labeling and quality check.
- **August through September**: Phase 2 renovation window when scheduled, completed before November brewing season.
- **August through October**: Seasonal brewer hiring pipeline open in Greenhouse.
- **Late September**: Barley harvest inspection with Dave Caldwell at his 200 acres.

### Annual

- **Every 6 months**: Dentist appointment with Dr. Mitchell at Asheville Smile Dental.
- **September**: Annual physical with Dr. Karen Albright at Asheville Family Clinic.
- **Mid-May**: Asheville Craft Beer Festival. Bates Brewing Company exhibits, Larry pours and networks.
- **Early June**: Great American Beer Festival submission deadline.
- **July 11**: Hana's birthday. Family celebration at home.
- **Early October**: Great American Beer Festival results announced.
- **November 28**: Larry's birthday.
- **December 31**: New Year's Eve bonfire and midnight toast with the family, anniversary ale poured.
