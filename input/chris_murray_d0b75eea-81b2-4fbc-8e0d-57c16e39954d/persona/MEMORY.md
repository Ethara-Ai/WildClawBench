# Memory — Chris Murray

## Personal Profile
- **Name**: Chris James Murray (clients call him Chris or Mr. Chris; his housekeeping team calls him Mr. Murray or just Boss; family calls him Chris; his mother calls him Chrissy)
- **Age**: 44
- **Location**: Las Vegas, NV (Spring Valley neighborhood), Pacific Time
- **Ethnicity**: Mexican American
- **Occupation**: Housekeeping supervisor at the Grand Sierra Hotel, Las Vegas Strip; owner and operator of Momentos by Chris, Quinceañera and milestone event planning
- **Education**: High school diploma from Crestview High School (2000); Hospitality Management Certificate from Southern Nevada Technical College (2014); Event Planning Certificate from Silverado Continuing Education Center (2019)

## Interests & Hobbies
- Event planning — has an eye for color, knows how to stretch a budget without making it look stretched
- Cooking — regular home-cooked dishes: burgers, mac and cheese, mashed potatoes, meatloaf, pot roast on Sundays; makes baked goods for holidays and sells cookie boxes by the dozen during the Christmas season as extra income
- Backyard entertaining — grilling and yard games at family parties; he taught himself woodworking from YouTube videos; works on projects in the garage with his kids; hasn't been to a bar in years but fires up the grill when the mood strikes
- TV viewing — watches sports reruns and whatever's on ESPN; it's his wind-down ritual after the kids are settled; he and Yolanda text commentary in real time
- Catholic faith — attends Mass at Our Lady of Las Vegas every Sunday
- Gardening — small container garden on the apartment patio: tomatoes, basil, peppers, and a rosemary bush
- Community organizing — involved with the Las Vegas Community Association; helps coordinate neighborhood block parties, bake sale fundraisers, and community events

## Key Relationships
- **Rosa Murray (mother)** — 68, lives in Jacksonville, Florida. Retired seamstress. The center of Chris's emotional world — they talk every Sunday for at least an hour by phone. Rosa worries about Chris working too hard and not remarrying. Chris sends $400/month to help with expenses.
- **Liam Murray (father, deceased)** — Irish American; married Rosa in 1985; the family carried his surname.
- **Carlos Murray (brother)** — 38, electrician in Jacksonville, married to Daniela (35). Two kids: Mateo (10) and Valentina (7). Carlos is dependable and helps look after Rosa. He and Chris talk every other week.
- **Sofia Murray (daughter)** — 21, senior at Silver State University studying nursing. Lives at home to save money. Sofia is serious, driven, and the first in the family to attend university. She helps Chris with event planning when she can. Their relationship is close but occasionally tense — Sofia wants Chris to slow down.
- **Miguel Murray (son)** — 18, senior at Crestview High School. Plans to attend Southern Nevada Technical College in the fall for automotive technology. Quiet, responsible, works part-time at Midas Auto Parts. He's the peacekeeper in the family.
- **Isabella Murray (daughter)** — 14, freshman at Crestview High School. The social one — dramatic, creative, already planning her own Quinceañera for next year. She wants to be a makeup artist. Chris worries about her getting distracted from school.
- **Ernesto Morton (ex-husband)** — 46, returned to Florida in 2016 after the divorce. Works in construction in Jacksonville. Child support is sporadic — Chris stopped relying on it years ago. The kids have complicated feelings; Sofia is angry, Miguel is distant, Isabella barely remembers him living in the house.
- **Yolanda Reynolds (best friend)** — 47, housekeeper at the Grand Sierra Hotel (Chris's team). They started at the hotel the same year. Yolanda is loud, loyal, and the person Chris calls when he needs to laugh or cry. They share a cigarette break that's more therapy than smoke.
- **Father Frank Harris (parish priest)** — 58, priest at Our Lady of Las Vegas. Born in Guatemala, ministered in Florida before coming to Las Vegas. Chris trusts his counsel and he knows his family situation.
- **Carmen Davidson (event business partner)** — 40, freelance event photographer who works with Chris on most events. They complement each other — Carmen handles the visual documentation while Chris runs the production. They've become close friends through the work.
- **Mr. Gerald Patterson (hotel general manager)** — 55, has been GM for 8 years. Professional relationship — he respects Chris's reliability, but Chris knows the GM doesn't fully understand what his team deals with. He picks his battles with Patterson carefully.
- **Mary Jensen (event choreographer)** — 32, dance instructor who teaches the waltz and surprise dance for Chris's Quinceañera clients. Reliable, creative, and good with nervous teenagers.

## Health & Wellness
- **Physical health**: Functional but wearing down from years of physical labor
  - Chronic lower back pain — from 19 years of housekeeping work: lifting, bending, pushing carts; he manages with ibuprofen and a heating pad; Dr. Reeves has recommended physical therapy and he hasn't gone
  - Right knee: intermittent pain, especially after long shifts or standing through Quinceañera events; he wraps it with a compression sleeve and keeps going
  - Elevated cholesterol — LDL 155 at last check (September 2025); Dr. Reeves wants him on statins; Chris is resisting because his mother managed it with diet and he wants to try that first
  - Annual physical at Spring Valley Medical Center (Dr. Patricia Reeves) — last visit September 2025; blood pressure borderline (138/86), weight stable, cholesterol flagged
  - Dental: overdue — last visit was February 2025; scheduling is hard with his hours
- **Mental health**: Resilient but stretched thin
  - The weight of being sole provider for three kids plus supporting his mother in Florida is constant — he doesn't complain but the math is always running in his head
  - Ernesto leaving in 2016 forced him to build everything alone; the anger has faded into a practical indifference, but Isabella's upcoming Quinceañera is stirring feelings about what a father should be present for
  - Loneliness he doesn't name — he hasn't dated since Ernesto; between work and the kids, there's no time, and he's not sure he trusts the process anymore
  - Yolanda and Father Frank are his emotional supports; Sofia increasingly fills that role too, which Chris feels guilty about because Sofia should be living her own life
  - No therapist — "Therapy is for people with time"
- **Exercise**: Housekeeping is his exercise — 8 hours of pushing carts, lifting mattresses, bending, walking miles of hallway; he walks the neighborhood in the evening when it's cool enough; dances in the kitchen
- **Sleep**: 9:30 PM - 4:45 AM weekdays (early shift); 10:30 PM - 7:00 AM weekends; falls asleep fast from exhaustion; the back pain wakes him sometimes
