# Memory — Lee Powers

## Personal Profile
- **Full Name:** Lee James Powers
- **Age:** 33 (born Sat, July 18, 1992)
- **Location:** Bend, OR (Pacific Time)
- **Ethnicity:** White American (Anglo)
- **Languages:** English (native)
- **Religion:** Protestant — attends Westside Community Church sporadically but prays daily and keeps a small wooden cross from his grandfather on his nightstand. Faith is personal and private, not institutional.
- **Education:** BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing) from Cascade Ridge University (2014). Emergency Nursing Certification (CEN) obtained in 2018. Currently considering pursuing a Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP) master's degree but hasn't committed due to time and cost.
- Lee was born in Spokane, Washington. His mother Barbara raised him alone after his father left when Lee was 3. They moved to Bend when Lee was 7, following his aunt Carol who had already settled there. He grew up in a working-class neighborhood on the south side of Bend. Never had a relationship with his father — the man left and never came back, and Barbara raised him alone. Lee was the first in his family to attend college.

## Interests & Hobbies
- **West Coast Swing dancing and instruction:** Lee's passion outside nursing. He started dancing swing at 15 at community events and took formal training in his 20s. Now teaches 3-4 classes per week at Tempo Swing Studio — beginner, intermediate, and a popular "West Coast Swing Fusion" class. He choreographs his own routines and occasionally performs at swing dance showcases and festivals.
- **Lindy Hop and East Coast Swing:** Dances socially but doesn't teach. Hits swing socials at clubs and community events.
- **Fitness:** Runs 3 times a week (usually 3-4 miles along the Deschutes River Trail). Does yoga on rest days — follows online classes. The nursing-and-dancing combo keeps him in good shape.
- **Grilling and smoking meat:** Learned from Barbara. Specializes in brisket, pulled pork, smoked ribs, and cornbread. Sunday cookouts with his mom are a ritual. Also makes a killer peach cobbler for friends' birthdays.
- **Music curation:** Obsessive about building playlists — for dance classes, for running, for cooking. Knows the swing genre deeply — from classic (Count Basie, Duke Ellington) to modern neo-swing and West Coast Swing DJs.
- **Journaling:** Keeps a journal — mostly processes the hard ER shifts. Doesn't share it with anyone.
- **Netflix/streaming:** Guilty pleasure — watches action thrillers, true crime docuseries, and the occasional comedy. De-stresses with TV after night shifts.
