# FNP Program Costs (clipped by Lee)

Saved notes on Family Nurse Practitioner master's programs I keep weighing. Read-only, applied to nothing.

## OHSU MSN-FNP
- Tuition: ~$65,000 total
- Length: 3 years part-time
- Format: hybrid, some Portland on-campus intensives

## Frontier Nursing University (online FNP)
- Tuition: ~$50,000 total
- Length: 2.5 years
- Format: fully online + clinical placements

## Gonzaga MSN-FNP
- Tuition: ~$58,000 total
- Length: 3 years
- Format: online with on-campus residencies

Tradeoff: cheapest and most flexible is Frontier, but clinical placement logistics around the ER schedule are the real cost.
