# CEU Options for CEN Recert (clipped by Lee)

Saved vendor options for the Emergency Nursing (CEN) continuing-education hours. Read-only, enrolled in nothing.

## BCEN / ENA Marketplace
- Cost: ~$300 for a full recert CEU bundle
- Format: self-paced online modules
- Covers: full CEN renewal hour requirement

## Nurse.com CEU Library
- Cost: ~$50/yr unlimited subscription
- Format: on-demand courses
- Covers: most, verify CEN-specific tagging

## Local hospital in-service credits
- Cost: free with shifts
- Format: in-person
- Covers: partial, supplements the online hours

Cheapest path: Nurse.com subscription + free in-service to top up.
