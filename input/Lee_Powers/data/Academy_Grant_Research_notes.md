# Academy Grant Notes (clipped by Lee)

Saved research on grants the Academy might qualify for. Read-only notes, nothing applied for yet.

## Oregon First-Generation Small Business Grant  <-- nearest deadline
- Amount: up to $10,000
- For: first-generation founders, Oregon-registered small businesses
- Deadline: current quarter application window, closing soonest of the lot
- Fit: strong (first-gen founder, Bend OR studio)
- Needs: reconciled cash position + simple budget narrative

## Bend Chamber Micro-Enterprise Grant
- Amount: up to $5,000
- For: Deschutes County small businesses under 5 employees
- Deadline: rolling, reviewed quarterly
- Fit: good

## Oregon Arts & Culture Recovery Grant
- Amount: up to $7,500
- For: arts/dance/creative-sector small operators
- Deadline: next cycle, further out
- Fit: medium (dance instruction qualifies)
