sunday pot roast (rosas way)
- 3 lb chuck, salt the night before
- sear it hard in the dutch oven, dont rush
- onions, carrots, celery, garlic
- beef broth + a splash of red wine, couple bay leafs
- 300 oven, about 3.5 hrs covered
- potatos go in the last hour
- big sprig of rosemary from the yard

serve after the 12 mass. sofia wants extra gravy again.
