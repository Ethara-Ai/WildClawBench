# Memory: Lamar Cochran

## Personal Profile

Lamar is a Black Swedish hockey coach from Skellefteå, head of the J20 program at Björkleden IF in Umeå. He played at a high level, including two junior seasons in North America, before retiring and moving into coaching. The transition was harder than he admitted at the time. He has stopped trying to hide that, and now uses the experience to coach players who will have to make their own version of that transition.

**Education and credentials**: Kandidatexamen (Bachelor's) in Sports Science from Norrland University, 2017. Swedish Hockey Federation Level 3 coaching certification, 2022. Currently pursuing the federation's advanced coaching diploma.

**Languages**: Swedish (native, distinctly northern Västerbotten cadence), English (fluent, with North American traces from the junior years in Canada), Norwegian (understands well by mutual intelligibility), basic Finnish from rink-side absorption.

**Citizenship and identity**: Swedish citizen, born and raised in Skellefteå, family from Västerbotten county. Culturally a Black Swedish man in a profession and a region where there are not many of him. He carries both halves of that identity without trying to make the gap invisible.

**Temperament**: Steady, contained intensity. Direct without being blunt, demanding without being harsh, warm in the specific places he chooses to be warm. He raises his voice once or twice a season, and the players have learned that the rarity is the point.

**Philosophy**: The ice reveals character. Coaching is recognizing the version of a player that only the pressure of competition shows, and developing it without breaking the person it lives inside. Development beats results, with the professional cost understood. He has been writing this down for a year in a personal coaching philosophy document.

**Faith**: Loosely Lutheran by way of Sweden, loosely Christian by way of the African-American tradition his mother grew up in. He prays before games sometimes. He has not been to a service in years and has made peace with the inconsistency.

**Cultural texture**: Speaks Swedish like a native of Västerbotten. Reads Black American literature he discovered late, including Baldwin, Whitehead, and Coates. Cooks his mother's food on Sundays with the rigor he learned as an athlete. Keeps northern Swedish weather habits and African-American cultural threads alongside each other without needing to choose.

**Loyalty**: Stays in touch with players he coached three years ago. Has refused at least one job offer because the timing would have asked him to abandon a player mid-development. He would describe this as the only correct decision.

## Work & Projects

**Employer**: Björkleden IF, Umeå. Head Coach, J20 Nationell (under-20 national junior league).

**Work focus**: Player development, tactical systems, game management, and building a J20-to-SHL pipeline. On-ice coaching, practice planning, game prep, and player evaluations. Bus trips across Sweden for away fixtures.

**Active projects**:
- **Playoff push**: J20 team currently 4th in their group with the regular season closing out. Realistic playoff target. Fine-tuning special teams and managing minutes for draft-eligible players.
- **SHL prospects**: Three J20 players identified as SHL-ready. Individual development plans, extra ice, coordination with the senior coaching staff on transition.
- **Summer camp**: Off-season development camp (June and July). On-ice and off-ice programming. Budget proposal due in February each year to Mikael.
- **Coaching philosophy document**: A personal long-form draft on player development, leadership, and tactical systems. Slow progress, ongoing.
- **Advanced coaching diploma**: Federation modules through the year. Coursework on the schedule.

**Schedule shape**: Practice Monday through Friday, typically 9 to 11 AM ice. Video, meetings, and planning fill afternoons. Games Friday and Saturday, occasionally midweek. Pre-season camp in mid-August, Nordic Coaching Symposium in early August.
