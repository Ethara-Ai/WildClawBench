# Heartbeat: Lamar Cochran

## Recurring Events

### Daily

- **Daily, 6:00 AM**: Wake. Strong filter coffee, first of four. Check overnight messages on phone before getting up.
- **Daily, 10:30 PM**: Bed. Audiobook on a timer if winding down after a late game.

### Weekly (Weekdays)

Monday and Tuesday start with a 6:30 AM gym session, followed by practice 9:00 to 11:00 AM and afternoons split between video review, player development, and scouting prep; Tuesday closes with dinner with Jasmine. Wednesday holds the 2:00 PM coaching staff meeting with Nils and the goaltending coach. Thursday runs a lighter, game-prep practice and often the monthly Tommy Svensson call. Friday is game day: morning skate at 10:00 AM, pre-game at 5:00 PM, puck drop at 7:00 PM, post-game debrief.

Saturday is game day if Friday was travel, otherwise a light morning gym and recovery or time with Jasmine. Sunday is meal prep, cross-country skiing or a long walk, an afternoon call with the parents in Skellefteå, and a 6:00 PM weekly review before an early night.

### Monthly

- **1st of each month**: Financial review. Savings transfer, investment check, expense review with Jasmine.
- **Last Friday of each month**: Coaching staff working dinner. Nils, the goaltending coach, the athletic trainer.
- **Once a month, often Thursday**: Phone call with Tommy Svensson in Skellefteå. Coaching philosophy.

### Quarterly

- **Quarterly**: Physiotherapy block review with Jonas Eriksson. Sessions every three weeks across the quarter.
- **Quarterly**: Federation advanced coaching diploma module check-in.

### Seasonal / Variable

- **Pre-season, mid-August**: Pre-season camp begins. Fitness testing, roster evaluation, system installation.
- **Regular season, September through April**: J20 games typically Friday and Saturday evenings, occasionally midweek.
- **Playoffs, early May**: J20 playoffs begin if the team qualifies.
- **Winter weekends, weather permitting**: Cross-country skiing in the Vindeln forest, solo, long loops.
- **Off-season, May through August**: Summer camp coordination, individual development calls, and the postponed Norway trip whenever the calendar allows.

### Annual

- **September**: Annual checkup with Dr. Anna Lindqvist at Umeå Hälsocentral.
- **Every six months**: Dental check with Dr. Erik Forsberg.
- **Early August**: Nordic Hockey Coaching Symposium in Gothenburg, three days.
- **February**: Summer camp budget and program proposal due to Mikael Johansson.
- **Late May**: End-of-season team banquet at Umeå Kulturhus.
- **October 10**: Tyrone's birthday. Family dinner in Skellefteå.
- **January 19**: Lamar's birthday. Jasmine usually organizes a small cabin trip.
- **December 24**: Cochran family Christmas in Skellefteå. Travel up the day before.
