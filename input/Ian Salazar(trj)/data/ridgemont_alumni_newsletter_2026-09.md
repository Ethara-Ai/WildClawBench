## Class Notes

- **Class of 2001** — Renee Atwater opened a community bookstore in Bend, Oregon. Story circle every Thursday.
- **Class of 2005** — Marcus Halberd is teaching middle school science in Pueblo, Colorado.
- **Class of 2006** — Daniela Kerns released a documentary short about acequia systems in northern New Mexico. Streaming on Kanopy through the local library.
- **Class of 2009** — Jeff Owusu and Priya Mehta were married in June at the Lava Beds. Both met in Dr. Kepner's geomorphology field camp.
- **Class of 2012** — Sara Ngozi was promoted to senior cartographer at the Sandia Mountain Land Trust.
- **Class of 2015** — Roberto Cienfuegos opened a coffee roastery in Las Vegas, NM. Open mics first Friday of the month.
