Raj's Competitor Notes - Stepper Research
Date: October 8, 2026

Looking at stepper patterns across portals for my branched file
(FK_PPR26A_stepper).

MyHealthHub: Their stepper uses a vertical accordion. Each step
expands inline. No breadcrumb visible during the flow. The
breadcrumb nav Anita noted is only on the main dashboard, not
in any transaction flow.

CareNav: Classic horizontal stepper with numbered circles. The
"inline refill tracker" is actually a progress bar, not a stepper.
I'd call it a progress indicator pattern, not an inline tracker.

WellSync: No stepper at all. They use a single long-form page
with section anchors. The "contextual help tooltips" are just
standard HTML title attributes, nothing special.

MediTrack: Best stepper implementation. Vertical with sub-steps
and validation at each stage. This is what I'm modeling my
frames 12:01, 12:14, 12:27 after.

Recommendation: We should adopt MediTrack's stepper pattern for
the refill flow. It's more robust than CareNav's approach.
