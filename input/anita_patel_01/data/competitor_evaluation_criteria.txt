Competitive Portal Audit - Evaluation Criteria
Project: PPR-26A Patient Portal Redesign
Version: Post-September re-scope (3 portals)

In-scope competitors (confirmed after mid-September cut):
  1. MyHealthHub Portal
  2. CareNav Portal
  3. WellSync Patient Portal

Evaluation dimensions per competitor:
  - Navigation persistence: Does the portal maintain wayfinding cues during multi-step flows?
  - Step progress visibility: Can users see where they are in a sequential process?
  - Contextual assistance: Are help elements available without leaving the current view?
  - Error recovery: How does the portal handle session interruptions during auth or data entry?

Each competitor finding should map to at least one PPR-26A Figma component and one Notion audit entry.
Pattern names should use descriptive labels (e.g. "persistent breadcrumb nav" rather than generic terms).

Photographic evidence: Anita snapped and annotated printouts of all three portals during on-site visits.
These annotation photos are stored as competitor_annotations.pdf in the project data folder.

Scoring: Findings ranked by Notion severity (high, medium, low), then by number of corroborating evidence sources descending.
