# Where to eat after sessions (Bushwick / Bed-Stuy / Crown Heights)

Notes for myself. Not related to any client work. Mostly weeknight reach.

## Bushwick — walking distance
- **Roberta's** — the obvious one, always packed Friday
- **Sally** — the cocktail bar with the small menu, kitchen closes 11
- **Mama Fox** — solid late dinner, gets loud
- **Foster Sundry** — sandwich for lunch days

## Bed-Stuy
- **Saraghina** — pizza, weekend brunch is the move
- **Speedy Romeo** — also pizza, faster
- **L'Antagoniste** — splurge dinner. Reserve two weeks out.

## Crown Heights
- **Win Son Bakery** — breakfast egg sandwich, before sessions
- **Glady's** — jerk chicken, post-mix night
- **Ras Plant Based** — when Taylor's around

## Coffee — fast
- Variety (Graham Ave)
- Little Skips (Willoughby)
- Sey Coffee (if I have time)

## Quick weeknight @ home
- Salmon + rice + roasted broccoli (15 min)
- The white-bean stew Mom taught me
- Cheap miso ramen pack + frozen dumplings + scallions
