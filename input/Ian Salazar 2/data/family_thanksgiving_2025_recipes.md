# Thanksgiving 2025 - Recipe File

Last year's menu, saved by Carmen. Keep for 2026 reference.

## Abuela Rosa's red chile tamales

- 5 lb pork shoulder, cubed and slow-cooked with garlic, oregano, salt
- 12 dried New Mexico red chile pods, stemmed, soaked, blended with soaking water and 4 garlic cloves until smooth
- 4 lb masa harina (Maseca) mixed with warm broth and 1 cup lard until soft
- 2 packages corn husks, soaked 1 hour
- Steam upright 90 minutes, rest 20 minutes

Rosa's note: do not skimp on the lard or they go tough.

## Marco's smoked turkey

- 14 lb bird, brined 18 hours in apple cider + kosher salt + bay
- Rubbed with smoked paprika, brown sugar, cumin
- Pecan wood at 275F, ~30 min/lb until breast hits 162F
- Rest 30 min tented

Marco said next year try mesquite half-and-half.

## Sofia's calabacitas

- 4 medium zucchini, diced
- 2 cups fresh corn (or frozen if December)
- 1 onion, half a can of fire-roasted green chile, garlic
- Cotija crumbled on top

## Diego's request (he was 7)

- Mac and cheese, the boxed kind, no substitutions

## Pies

- Pumpkin (Rosa)
- Pecan (store-bought from Pic Quik, we agreed not to mention this on the table)
